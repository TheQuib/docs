## Waiting to send

Lists email still in the queue. Notifications usually send within seconds.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Send now | Sends the queue, and says why if it can’t. |
| Cancel email | Stops a waiting notification from sending. |

## Activity list

Lists every email sent and received, newest first. Filter by **Direction** and status, or search by address, subject, or error.

Choose **Needs attention** to show only failures and refused email.
