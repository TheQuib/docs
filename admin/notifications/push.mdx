---
title: "Push notifications"
description: "Notify people on their phones and computers."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Admins turn it on. Each person then turns it on for each device.  
  **Where:** Settings → Notifications → Push notifications
</Info>

{/* Screenshot: Settings → Notifications, Push notifications */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Send push notifications">
    Then select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask people to turn it on">
    See [Install the app](/using/install-app).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Push follows the same rules as email. Message text is not included.
