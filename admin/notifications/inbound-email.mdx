## Before you start

- Connect [outbound email](/admin/notifications/outbound-email).
- Get your inbound address from whoever set up this help desk.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Receive email at the inbound address">
    Then select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what happens to new email">
    With **Open a new ticket from email that is not a reply** on, new email opens a ticket. With it off, only replies are accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Reply-to address">
    Under **Outbound email**, enter your inbound address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">
    Email the inbound address from an outside address, then reply to the notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  The email opens a ticket, and your reply appears on it.
</Check>

## How email is handled

| Email | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A reply to a notification | Added to the ticket. |
| A reply to a resolved or closed ticket | Reopens the ticket. |
| From a domain listed on a client | The ticket is assigned to that client. |
| From someone without an account | Opens a ticket. It moves to their account when they get one. |
| Auto-replies and bounces | Ignored, and listed in Email activity. |
