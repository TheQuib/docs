## Invitation expiry

Unused invitations deactivate the account and email the person and whoever invited them.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deactivate accounts whose invitation goes unused | Turns expiry on or off. | On |
| Days before an invitation expires | How long a link stays unused before the account is deactivated. | 14 |

Accounts added without an invitation email never expire.

## Resend an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person">
    In **Settings → Users**, select them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Resend invitation">
    They receive a new link. An expired account is reactivated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Revoke an invitation

Under **Pending invitations**, select **Revoke**.
