---
title: "Add people"
description: "Invite someone, or add them without an email."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Admins add anyone. Staff add clients.  
  **Where:** Settings → Users
</Info>

{/* Screenshot: Settings → Users, Add someone */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Add someone">
    Staff see **Add a client**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Enter their **Email** and choose a **Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to add them">
    **Email them an invitation now** sends a link to set a password. With it off, select **Add without emailing**. They can then sign in with **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  They appear in the list, under their client or your team.
</Check>

Accounts at a domain listed on a client are assigned to that client. See [Clients](/admin/clients).
