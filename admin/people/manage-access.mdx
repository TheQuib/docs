---
title: "Manage access"
description: "Change what someone can do, or remove their access."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Admins  
  **Where:** Settings → Users
</Info>

{/* Screenshot: Settings → Users, edit dialog */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    Search by name or email. Under **Show**, choose **Never signed in** or **Deactivated** to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select them">
    Their details open in a dialog. Changes save as you make them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Use the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save">
    The dialog closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Client raises and tracks their own tickets. Staff works all tickets. Administrator has full access. |
| Client | The organization they belong to. |
| Location | Where they work. |
| All client tickets | Lets a client see and reply on every ticket for their client. |
| Resend invitation | Sends a new link. Reactivates an account whose invitation expired. |
| Deactivate | Stops them signing in. **Reactivate** reverses it. |
| Delete | Deletes the account permanently. Their tickets and messages are kept. |
