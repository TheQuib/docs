| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At risk at | The share of a target used before it counts as at risk. | 75% |

Choose who receives at-risk and missed emails in [Who gets notified](/admin/notifications/who-gets-notified). Requesters never receive them.
