---
title: "Turn SLAs off"
description: "Turn SLAs off, and back on."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Admins  
  **Where:** Settings → SLAs
</Info>

## Turn off

Select **Turn off**. Clocks are hidden and no reminders are sent. Settings are kept.

## Turn back on

| Select | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Turn on | Restores your settings. |
| Run setup again | Opens setup with your settings filled in. |
