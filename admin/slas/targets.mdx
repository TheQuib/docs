---
title: "Targets"
description: "Set first response and resolution targets for each priority."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Admins  
  **Where:** Settings → SLAs → Targets
</Info>

{/* Screenshot: Settings → SLAs, Targets */}

Enter a number, then choose hours or days. Blank means no target. Targets count business hours unless **24/7** is on.

| Priority | First response | Resolution | 24/7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | 1 hour | 4 hours | On |
| High | 4 hours | 1 business day | Off |
| Normal | 1 business day | 3 business days | Off |
| Low | 2 business days | 5 business days | Off |

These are the defaults. You can’t add priorities or set targets for one client.
