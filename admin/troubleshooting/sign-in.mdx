## Invitations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The invitation didn’t arrive">
    Check [Email activity](/admin/notifications/email-activity), then the person’s spam folder. To send another, select **Resend invitation** on their account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invitation link expired">
    Select **Resend invitation** on their account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The link says it was already used">
    A mail scanner may have opened it. Ask them to use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The account shows as deactivated">
    Its invitation may have expired. Select **Resend invitation** to reactivate it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The account was created, but the invitation wasn’t sent">
    Fix your [outbound email](/admin/notifications/outbound-email), then select **Resend invitation**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Signing in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Someone forgot their password">
    Ask them to select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The password reset didn’t arrive">
    Check [Email activity](/admin/notifications/email-activity). No entry means the address has no active account, or they asked too many times. Try again in an hour.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
