---
title: "Email troubleshooting"
description: "Fix problems with outbound and inbound email."
---

Start in [Email activity](/admin/notifications/email-activity). It shows each email, its status, and any error.

## Outbound

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Nothing sends">
    Check that **Send notification emails** is on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The connection fails or times out">
    Check the port and SSL/TLS: 465 with **Use SSL/TLS** on, or 587 with it off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The test works, but notifications don’t send">
    Open **Waiting to send** and select **Send now**. It says why if it can’t send. Contact whoever set up this help desk.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Email lands in spam">
    Set up SPF and DKIM on the sending domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Microsoft 365 says the client secret is invalid">
    You may have entered the **Secret ID**. Create a new secret and enter its **Value**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Microsoft 365 says the secret expired">
    Create a new secret and enter its **Value**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Microsoft 365 says you aren’t allowed to send">
    Check the Exchange permission covers the mailbox. It can take up to two hours to apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Microsoft 365 can’t find the mailbox">
    Use the mailbox’s primary address as the **From address**, not an alias.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Inbound

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Email to the inbound address does nothing">
    Check that **Receive email at the inbound address** is on, then check Email activity. Contact whoever set up this help desk.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replies open new tickets">
    Set the **Reply-to address** to your inbound address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Senders are told the address only accepts replies">
    **Open a new ticket from email that is not a reply** is off. Turn it on to accept new email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your organization stopped receiving other email">
    Contact whoever set up this help desk.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
