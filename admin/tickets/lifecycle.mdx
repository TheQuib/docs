---
title: "Lifecycle"
description: "Set when resolved tickets close."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Admins  
  **Where:** Settings → Tickets → Lifecycle
</Info>

{/* Screenshot: Settings → Tickets, Lifecycle */}

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close resolved tickets automatically | Closes tickets after the wait. When off, tickets stay Resolved until staff close them. | On |
| Days to wait | Counted from when the ticket was marked resolved. | 7 |

A reply reopens a resolved or closed ticket.
