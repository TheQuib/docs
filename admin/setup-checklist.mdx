---
title: "Setup checklist"
description: "Get your help desk ready for its first ticket."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Admins
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your branding">
    Add your name, color, and logo. See [Branding](/admin/branding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect outbound email">
    Connect your mail server and send a test. See [Outbound email](/admin/notifications/outbound-email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add clients and locations">
    Add the organizations you support and their locations. See [Clients](/admin/clients) and [Locations](/admin/locations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up ticket categories">
    Add the categories people choose from. See [Categories](/admin/tickets/categories).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Add your staff and other admins. See [Add people](/admin/people/add-people).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets notified">
    Choose who receives email and push for each event. See [Who gets notified](/admin/notifications/who-gets-notified).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up SLAs (optional)">
    Set targets and business hours. See [Set up SLAs](/admin/slas/set-up).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on inbound email (optional)">
    Open tickets from email. See [Inbound email](/admin/notifications/inbound-email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise a test ticket">
    Raise a ticket as a client, then reply as staff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your help desk is ready when the client receives the reply by email.
</Check>
