{/* Screenshot: Ticket page */}

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Conversation | Every message, newest at the bottom. Internal notes are marked. |
| Reply box | Reply, add an internal note, and log time. |
| Requester | Who raised it, their email, client, and location. |
| Details | Status, priority, assignee, category, client, and location. |
| SLA | First response and resolution clocks. |
| Time | Time logged on the ticket. |
| Email history | Email sent and received on the ticket. |

On a phone, select **Details** to open the side cards.
