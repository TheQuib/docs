## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | New, or waiting on your team. |
| In progress | Your team is working on it. Set by hand. |
| Awaiting reply | Waiting on the requester. |
| On hold | Paused. |
| Resolved | Fixed. |
| Closed | Finished. |

A public staff reply moves an Open ticket to Awaiting reply. A reply reopens a resolved or closed ticket.

## Priorities

Low, Normal, High, and Urgent. Priority sets the [SLA target](/admin/slas/targets).
