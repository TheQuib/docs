title: "Sign in"
description: "Set your password, sign in, or reset your password."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Everyone
</Info>

## First sign-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Select the link in the email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a password">
    Use at least 8 characters, then select **Save password and continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Forgot password?">
    It’s on the sign-in screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Select **Send reset link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    Choose a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The link has expired or was already used">
    Request a new one with **Forgot password?**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My invitation expired">
    Ask your help desk admin to resend it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
