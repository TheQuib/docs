---

<Info>
  **Who:** Everyone  
  **Where:** Tickets → New ticket
</Info>

{/* Screenshot: New ticket form */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select New ticket">
    It’s on the Tickets page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Enter a **Title** and **Description**, and choose a **Category** and **Priority**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add files">
    Optional. Each file can be up to 25 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create ticket">
    Your ticket opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You receive an email confirming your ticket.
</Check>

Staff can enter a requester’s email to raise a ticket for them.
