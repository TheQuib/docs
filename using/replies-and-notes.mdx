---
title: "Replies and internal notes"
description: "Reply to a requester, or leave a note for your team."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Staff and admins  
  **Where:** A ticket page
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your reply">
    Markdown is supported. Add files if you need to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Internal note, if needed">
    Internal notes aren’t shown to the requester or emailed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Use the table below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Send reply | Sends the reply. |
| Send and resolve | Sends the reply and resolves the ticket. |
| Send and close | Sends the reply and closes the ticket. It’s under **More send options**. |
| Send and reopen | Sends the reply and reopens a resolved or closed ticket. |
| Save note | Saves the internal note. The same options apply. |
