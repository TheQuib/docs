---
title: "Your account"
description: "Change your name, appearance, clock, password, and notifications."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Everyone  
  **Where:** Settings → Account
</Info>

{/* Screenshot: Settings → Account */}

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display name | Shown on your tickets and replies. Leave blank to use your email address. | Blank |
| Profile picture | Set on Gravatar. | Colored initials |
| App and notifications | Install the app and turn on push for this device. | Off |
| Appearance | Match this device, Always light, or Always dark. | Match this device |
| Clock | 12-hour or 24-hour. | 12-hour |
| Password | Enter at least 8 characters, then select **Change password**. | — |

Only an admin can change your **Email** or **Role**.
