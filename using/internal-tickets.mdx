---
title: "Internal tickets"
description: "Tickets only your team can see."
---

<Info>
  **Who:** Staff and admins  
  **Where:** Internal
</Info>

## Raise an internal ticket

On the new ticket form, check **Internal**.

## What is different

- Only staff and admins can see them.
- Nobody is emailed.
- They have no client and no SLA.
- Time logging works.

## Make a ticket internal

On a ticket page, select **Make internal**. Anything still waiting to send is canceled. You can’t make an internal ticket a client ticket.
