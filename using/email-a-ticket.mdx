---
title: "Raise a ticket by email"
description: "Open and reply to tickets from your inbox."
---

<Note>
  This works only if your help desk has inbound email on.
</Note>

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Open a ticket | Email your help desk’s support address. |
| Reply | Reply to the notification email. |
| Reopen a ticket | Reply to a resolved or closed ticket’s notification. |

If you’re told the address only accepts replies, [raise the ticket in desk](/using/raise-a-ticket).
